# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We’re dedicated to building a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone involved in this project — regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

We believe in creating a space where everyone feels safe, heard, and valued.

## Expected Behavior

As contributors and maintainers, we pledge to:

- Use inclusive, welcoming language
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Work towards the well-being of the community
- Show empathy and kindness to others

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following actions are not tolerated:

- Use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Sharing private information (like email or addresses) without permission
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate or unprofessional

## Our Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying what’s acceptable and will take appropriate action in response to any unacceptable behavior.

They reserve the right to edit, delete, or reject comments, commits, code, or other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct — and may ban any contributor if needed to maintain a safe environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both in project spaces and public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the maintainers at **icloud1990ch@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed and handled with discretion and confidentiality.
